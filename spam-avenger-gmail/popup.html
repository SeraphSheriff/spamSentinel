🛡️ Spam Avenger for Gmail

One click traces who really sent a spam email and writes the abuse complaint for you.

  1. Open a spam email in Gmail.
  2. Click the red shield button (bottom-right).
  3. Copy the complaint — or open it pre-addressed in Gmail — and send.

Button colors: ● green — Spam Avenger does the work; you just review and send. ● yellow — needs a few steps from you, and I'll guide you through them. ● orange — you're on your own with this one; no guide after the click.

Your details — for data-deletion demands

Any email in Gmail can also become a formal demand that the sender delete your personal data — the panel drafts it from these details, citing your state's privacy law.

Stored only in your browser, used only to fill in deletion demands you draft.

Nothing is sent automatically. Everything runs in your browser; the only lookups are the spammer's IP (RDAP + reverse DNS). Free, no account, no config. Desktop Chrome / Edge / Brave.
